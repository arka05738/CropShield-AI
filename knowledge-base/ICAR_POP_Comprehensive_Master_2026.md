# ICAR Package of Practices (POP) Comprehensive Master Guidelines (2026)
### Authoritative Crop Protection & Diagnostic Formulations
Published under guidelines of the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) & State Agricultural Universities.

---

## 1. Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)
- **Etiology**: *Alternaria solani* (Early Blight), *Phytophthora infestans* (Late Blight), *Bemisia tabaci* (Whitefly / TYLCV Vector), *Aphis gossypii* (Aphids).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Temperature 24–30°C with relative humidity > 80% and prolonged morning dew.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Prophylactic: Mancozeb 75% WP (Dithane M-45) @ 2.5 g/L water.
  - Curative: Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC (Amistar Top) @ 1.0 ml/L water.
  - Sucking Pests / Whiteflies: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.4 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.3 g/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 7 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Withhold excess Nitrogen (-30%); apply Potassium Sulphate (0:0:50) foliar spray @ 5 g/L to reinforce epidermal cell walls.
- **IPM Protocol**: Yellow sticky traps @ 15/acre; Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/L; drip irrigation to prevent foliar splash.

## 2. Rice / Paddy (*Oryza sativa*)
- **Etiology**: *Magnaporthe oryzae* (Foliar/Neck Blast), *Xanthomonas oryzae* (Bacterial Blight), *Scirpophaga incertulas* (Yellow Stem Borer), *Nilaparvata lugens* (BPH).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Intermittent drizzle, overcast days, relative humidity > 85%, temperature 22–28°C.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Rice Blast: Tricyclazole 75% WP (Beam / Baan) @ 0.6 g/L water or Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 1.5 ml/L.
  - Bacterial Leaf Blight: Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L + Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Stem Borer (Dead Hearts): Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC (Coragen) @ 0.3 ml/L or Cartap Hydrochloride 50 SP @ 2.0 g/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 30 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Avoid excess urea top-dressing during cloudy weather; apply Calcium Silicate @ 200 kg/ha basal.
- **IPM Protocol**: Drain field water for 24-48 hours; release *Trichogramma japonicum* @ 100,000/ha; pheromone traps @ 8/ha.

## 3. Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*)
- **Etiology**: Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV vectored by *Bemisia tabaci*), *Helicoverpa armigera* (American Bollworm), *Pectinophora gossypiella* (Pink Bollworm).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Bollworm Complex: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L or Flubendiamide 39.35 SC @ 0.2 ml/L.
  - Sucking Pests / Whiteflies: Diafenthiuron 50 WP @ 1.2 g/L or Pyriproxyfen 10 EC @ 2.0 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 15 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Spray 2% DAP or 1% 19:19:19 + Magnesium Sulphate 1% during square development.
- **IPM Protocol**: Marigold trap crops (1:16 ratio); HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha; Helilure pheromone traps @ 5/acre.

---

## 4. Potato (*Solanum tuberosum*)
- **Etiology**: *Phytophthora infestans* (Late Blight), *Phthorimaea operculella* (Potato Tuber Moth).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Protective: Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75 WP @ 2.0 g/L.
  - Curative / Systemic: Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Curzate M-8) @ 2.5 g/L or Dimethomorph 50 WP @ 1.0 g/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 10 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Adequate potassium (SOP) at earthing-up; avoid nitrogen after tuber initiation.
- **IPM Protocol**: Earthing-up to provide 10cm ridge soil cover; dehaulm 10 days before harvest if late blight is active.

## 5. Wheat (*Triticum aestivum*)
- **Etiology**: *Puccinia striiformis* (Yellow Stripe Rust), *Puccinia triticina* (Brown Leaf Rust), *Rhopalosiphum padi* (Wheat Aphid).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Stripe / Leaf Rust: Tebuconazole 25.9% EC (Folicur) @ 1.0 ml/L or Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1.0 ml/L.
  - Aphids: Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.2 g/L or Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.5 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 35 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Avoid late-stage urea top-dressing; ensure basal Zinc Sulphate 21% @ 25 kg/ha.

---

## 6. Maize (*Zea mays*)
- **Etiology**: *Spodoptera frugiperda* (Fall Armyworm), *Exserohilum turcicum* (Turcicum Leaf Blight).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Fall Armyworm: Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/L directed into central whorl.
  - Turcicum Leaf Blight: Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 14 days.
- **IPM Protocol**: Intercrop with cowpea or pigeonpea; apply bio-agent *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L; pheromone traps @ 5/acre.

---

## 7. Chilli (*Capsicum annuum*)
- **Etiology**: *Colletotrichum capsici* (Anthracnose / Dieback), *Scirtothrips dorsalis* (Chilli Thrips), Murda Virus.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Anthracnose: Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Tebuconazole 50% + Trifloxystrobin 25% WG @ 0.6 g/L.
  - Thrips: Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/L or Fipronil 5 SC @ 1.5 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 5 days.
- **IPM Protocol**: Blue sticky traps @ 15/acre; border rows of maize/sorghum; Calcium Nitrate 0.5% + Boron 0.1% spray.

---

## 8. Soybean (*Glycine max*)
- **Etiology**: *Phakopsora pachyrhizi* (Asian Soybean Rust), *Oberereopsis brevis* (Girdle Beetle).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Rust: Tebuconazole 10% + Sulphur 65% WG @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Girdle Beetle: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 21 days.
- **IPM Protocol**: Broad Bed Furrow (BBF) drainage; seed inoculation with *Bradyrhizobium japonicum*.

---

## 9. Sugarcane (*Saccharum officinarum*)
- **Etiology**: *Colletotrichum falcatum* (Red Rot), *Chilo infuscatellus* (Early Shoot Borer).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Sett Treatment: Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L sett dip for 15 minutes.
  - Shoot Borer: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/L furrow drenching.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 60 days.

---

## 10. Groundnut / Peanut (*Arachis hypogaea*)
- **Etiology**: *Cercospora arachidicola* (Early Leaf Spot), *Cercosporidium personatum* (Late Tikka Spot), *Spodoptera litura* (Tobacco Caterpillar).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Tikka Spot: Mancozeb 75% WP + Carbendazim 12% WP (Saaf / Companion) @ 2.0 g/L or Tebuconazole 25.9 EC @ 1.0 ml/L.
  - Spodoptera: Novaluron 10 EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 15 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Gypsum application @ 400 kg/ha at pegging stage (40-45 DAP) is crucial to prevent empty pods ("pops").
- **IPM Protocol**: Crop rotation with Pearl Millet/Sorghum; Spodolure pheromone traps @ 5/acre; SlNPV @ 250 LE/ha in evening.

---

## 11. Chickpea / Bengal Gram (*Cicer arietinum*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur
- **Etiology**: *Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *ciceri* (Fusarium Vascular Wilt), *Helicoverpa armigera* (Gram Pod Borer).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Daytime 22-26°C, relative humidity 65-75%, podding coincidence with clear warm weather.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Pod Borer: Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 0.3 ml/L or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG (Proclaim) @ 0.4 g/L.
  - Seedling Wilt / Root Rot: Seed dressing with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.0 g/kg seed.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 14 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Starter basal N (20 kg/ha) + SSP (40 kg P2O5/ha supplies 16% S); seed inoculation with *Mesorhizobium ciceri* and PSB liquid culture @ 10 ml/kg seed.
- **IPM Protocol**: Intercrop with Coriander or Mustard (4:1) or Marigold borders; erect bamboo bird perches @ 15-20/acre; HaNPV @ 250 LE/ha; Helilure traps @ 5/acre.

## 12. Mustard & Rapeseed (*Brassica juncea*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-Directorate of Rapeseed-Mustard Research (DRMR), Bharatpur
- **Etiology**: *Alternaria brassicae* (Alternaria Leaf & Pod Blight), *Lipaphis erysimi* (Mustard Aphid).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Cool cloudy weather (15-20°C) with morning fog and relative humidity > 75% in January-February.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Alternaria Blight: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.0 g/L or Iprodione 50% WP @ 2.0 g/L.
  - Mustard Aphid: Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Dimethoate 30% EC (Rogor) @ 1.5 ml/L applied when ETL exceeds 25-30 aphids/10 cm central twig.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 21 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Elemental Sulphur @ 40 kg/ha or Gypsum @ 250 kg/ha basal to bolster glucosinolate synthesis and cuticular defense.
- **IPM Protocol**: Early sowing between 10th-25th October to escape aphid build-up; yellow sticky traps @ 15/acre; spray *Verticillium lecanii* @ 5 g/L.

---

## 13. Onion (*Allium cepa*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research (DOGR), Rajgurunagar, Pune
- **Etiology**: *Alternaria porri* (Purple Blotch), *Thrips tabaci* (Onion Thrips).
- **Optimal Conditions**: High humidity (80-90%) with intermittent showers and warm temperature (25-30°C).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Purple Blotch: Difenoconazole 25% EC (Score) @ 1.0 ml/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1.0 ml/L.
  - Onion Thrips: Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 ml/L or Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/L.
  - Mandatory: Always blend non-ionic sticker-adjuvant (Sandovit @ 0.5 ml/L) to prevent aqueous run-off on waxy leaves.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 10 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Basal N:P:K (50:50:50 kg/ha) + Sulphur 30 kg/ha. Terminate nitrogen top-dressing after 60 days of transplanting to ensure bulb storability.
- **IPM Protocol**: Two outer barrier rows of Maize or Sorghum; blue & yellow sticky traps @ 20/acre; sprinkler irrigation suppresses thrips by 45%.

## 14. Banana (*Musa acuminata*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-National Research Centre for Banana (NRCB), Tiruchirappalli
- **Etiology**: *Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *cubense* (Tropical Race 4 / Panama Wilt), *Odoiporus longicollis* (Pseudostem Weevil).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Ill-drained waterlogged clay soils, warm humid tropical climate (26-34°C).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - TR4 Sanitation: Eradicate infected mats using 250 g bleaching powder per pit; corm injection with Carbendazim 2% solution (2 ml/plant).
  - Pseudostem Weevil: Swab lower stem up to 1 meter with Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2.5 ml/L or Carbaryl 50 WP @ 4.0 g/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 30 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: High Potassium (300 g K2O/plant in 4 splits); avoid ammoniacal nitrogen fertilizers which acidify rhizosphere and accelerate spore germination.
- **IPM Protocol**: Longitudinal split pseudostem traps @ 25/ha baited with cut banana pseudo-stems; rhizosphere enrichment with *Trichoderma viride* NRCB bio-formulation @ 50 g/plant.

## 15. Mango (*Mangifera indica*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-Central Institute for Subtropical Horticulture (CISH), Lucknow
- **Etiology**: *Oidium mangiferae* (Powdery Mildew), *Amritodus atkinsoni* (Mango Hopper).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Cool cloudy nights followed by sunny warm days during February-March panicle emergence.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Powdery Mildew: Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Wettable Sulphur 80% WDG @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Mango Hopper: Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L before full blossom.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 21 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Post-harvest balanced application (1000:500:1000 g NPK/tree for bearing orchards); foliar Boron 0.2% + Zinc 0.5% at marble stage.
- **IPM Protocol**: Post-harvest canopy pruning to improve airflow; green lacewing (*Chrysoperla carnea*) release @ 50-100/tree; solar light traps @ 1 trap/2 acres.

## 16. Apple (*Malus domestica*)
- **Authority**: SKUAST Kashmir & Dr YSP University of Horticulture & Forestry, Nauni
- **Etiology**: *Venturia inaequalis* (Apple Scab), *Panonychus ulmi* (European Red Mite).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Prolonged leaf wetness (> 9 hours) with temperature 15-20°C in temperate mountain zones.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Apple Scab (Pink Bud to Petal Fall): Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.3 ml/L or Captan 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - European Red Mite: Propergite 57% EC @ 1.0 ml/L or Fenazaquin 10% EC @ 1.5 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 28 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Urea 5% foliar spray on tree canopy immediately before autumn leaf fall accelerates fallen leaf decomposition and kills overwintering pseudothecia.
- **IPM Protocol**: Orchard floor sanitation and burning of leaf litter; conserve predatory phytoseiid mites (*Typhlodromus pyri*); corrugated trunk bands.

## 17. Grapes (*Vitis vinifera*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-National Research Centre for Grapes (NRCG), Pune (GrapeNet Compliance)
- **Etiology**: *Plasmopara viticola* (Downy Mildew), *Maconellicoccus hirsutus* (Grapevine Mealybug).
- **Optimal Conditions**: "Rule of 10-10-24" (10 mm rainfall, 10°C minimum temperature, in 24 hours).
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Downy Mildew Curative: Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L or Cyazofamid 34.5 SC @ 0.5 ml/L.
  - Mealybug: Spirotetramat 15.31% OD @ 0.7 ml/L or Buprofezin 25 SC @ 1.25 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 60 days (Strict export MRL adherence).
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Potassium Phosphonate (0.4%) foliar spray to induce Systemic Acquired Resistance (SAR); restrict late nitrogen flushes.
- **IPM Protocol**: Shoot positioning and bunch aeration; predatory Australian ladybird beetles (*Cryptolaemus montrouzieri*) @ 1500 beetles/ha; trunk sticky banding against ants.

## 18. Pomegranate (*Punica granatum*)
- **Authority**: ICAR-National Research Centre on Pomegranate (NRCP), Solapur
- **Etiology**: *Xanthomonas axonopodis* pv. *punicae* (Bacterial Blight / Oily Spot), *Deudorix isocrates* (Anar Butterfly / Fruit Borer).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Relative humidity > 85%, temperature 25-35°C, high-velocity rain splatters during monsoon.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Bacterial Blight: Streptomycin Sulphate 90% + Tetracycline Hydrochloride 10% (Streptocycline) @ 0.5 g/L + Copper Oxychloride 50 WP @ 2.5 g/L.
  - Fruit Borer: Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 20 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Enrich FYM with *Pseudomonas fluorescens* (10 kg/tree); foliar spray of Boron 0.1% + Zinc 0.25% + Calcium Nitrate 0.5% to resist peel cracking.
- **IPM Protocol**: Prune blight cankers 5 cm below infected marks followed by 10% Bordeaux paste; bag individual developing fruits with polypropylene non-woven bags (100% butterfly barrier).

## 19. Tea (*Camellia sinensis*)
- **Authority**: UPASI Tea Research Foundation, Valparai & Tea Research Association (TRA), Tocklai
- **Etiology**: *Exobasidium vexans* (Blister Blight), *Helopeltis theivora* (Tea Mosquito Bug).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Continuous mist, high humidity (> 90%), ambient temperature 18-24°C in high-elevation plantations.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Blister Blight: Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 0.4 ml/L + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 1.0 g/L (combination spray at 7-10 day intervals).
  - Tea Mosquito Bug: Bifenthrin 8% SC @ 1.0 ml/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.25 g/L.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 14 days (Strict compliance with Tea Board of India Plant Protection Code).
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: High Potash (N:K2O in 1:1 or 1:1.5 ratio) to strengthen leaf cuticular wax against basidiospore penetration.
- **IPM Protocol**: Lopping of shade trees before monsoon; short-round plucking (5-7 days) removes tender susceptible flush and bug eggs; spray *Beauveria bassiana* @ 3 g/L.

## 20. Coffee (*Coffea arabica* & *Coffea canephora*)
- **Authority**: Central Coffee Research Institute (CCRI), Coffee Board of India, Balehonnur
- **Etiology**: *Hemileia vastatrix* (Coffee Leaf Rust), *Xylotrechus quadripes* (Coffee White Stem Borer).
- **Optimal Conditions**: Intermittent rain showers, dense shade causing mist retention, temperature 21-28°C.
- **Chemical Prescription**:
  - Coffee Leaf Rust: Triadimefon 25% WP (Bayleton) @ 0.5 g/L or Bordeaux Mixture 0.5% (Pre- & Post-monsoon).
  - White Stem Borer: Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 3.0 ml/L mixed in 10% Agricultural Lime slurry for trunk swabbing.
- **Withholding Period (PHI)**: 30 days.
- **Fertilizer Recovery**: Balanced N:P2O5:K2O (40:30:40 kg/ha) post-monsoon; apply agricultural lime @ 500 kg/ha every 2-3 years to sustain soil pH at 6.0-6.5.
- **IPM Protocol**: Two-tier shade canopy regulation (40-50% sunlight); bark scrubbing with coir scrubbers to destroy borer eggs; CCRI cross-vane pheromone traps @ 25/ha; spray *Verticillium lecanii* @ 5 g/L.
